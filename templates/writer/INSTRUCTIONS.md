# `writer`

**Role:** turns a finished discussion into a document.
**Use when:** you agreed a plan in chat and want it as a spec, a summary or a decision record.
**Previous role:** `clarifier`.

**Change:** the section list. Replace it with your team's document format, for example an ADR or an RFC. Keep it short.
**Keep:** "do not interview" and "a gap becomes an open question".
**Test:** run it after a real discussion. A new person should know what to build from the output.
